# Task 33 — Catalogue editor shell: ReaImGui guard, draft store, entry list

Target: claude

## Goal
Implement the foundation of feature `catalogue-editor`: the "Catalogue
management" action, its ReaImGui guard, the persistent local draft with its
base, and the entry-list window the rest of the editor builds on.

## Acceptance criteria
- Without ReaImGui, the action explains that catalogue management needs
  ReaImGui (installable through ReaPack) and exits.
- On first open it fetches the catalogue and starts a draft from it,
  storing the fetched copy as the draft's base; the draft survives closing
  the window and restarting REAPER until published or discarded.
- The window lists every draft entry with name, vendor, platforms covered
  and activation method, marks entries added, changed or removed since the
  base, and shows validation problems beside their entry.
- Discard deletes the draft after confirmation; Publish is present and calls
  `catalogue-publish.publish(draft)` when that feature exists.
